Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Your Privacy Matters: Attendio is built with privacy at its core. All your attendance data stays on your device. We do not collect, access, transmit, or store any of your personal information on external servers.

This Privacy Policy explains how Attendio ("we," "us," or "the App") handles your information. By using Attendio, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information Collection

1.1 Personal Data You Provide

When you use Attendio, you may enter the following information:

  • Your name or identifier
  • Daily attendance status (Present, Absent, Half-Day, Overtime)
  • Salary information for calculation purposes
  • Work schedule and calendar data

All of this data is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this information.

1.2 Automatically Collected Data

Attendio does not automatically collect any data about your usage, device information, or behavior. The App operates entirely offline and does not communicate with external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since all data is stored locally on your device, we do not "use" your information in any way. Your data is processed entirely on your device to:

  • Display your attendance records in calendar format
  • Calculate estimated salary based on your attendance
  • Generate monthly statistics and insights
  • Provide the core functionality of the App

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All attendance data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by your operating system. This data never leaves your device through the App.

3.2 No Cloud Backup

Attendio does not provide cloud backup or sync features. Your data exists only on your device. If you uninstall the App or lose your device, your data may be lost unless you have a device-level backup system in place (such as Android backup services).

3.3 Security Measures

We rely on your device's security features to protect your data. We recommend:

  • Using a secure lock screen (PIN, password, fingerprint, or face recognition)
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Not rooting or jailbreaking your device, as this may compromise security

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your data to any third parties. Since your data never leaves your device through the App, there is nothing to share.

4.1 Third-Party Services

Attendio does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not contain ads and does not track your behavior.

4.2 App Store Information

When you download Attendio from the Google Play Store, Google may collect information according to their own privacy policy. We have no control over or access to this information. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

5. Children's Privacy

Attendio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the App, please contact us, though note that we have no access to any data stored locally on devices.

6. Your Data Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • Access: You can view all your data within the App at any time
  • Modification: You can edit or update your attendance records and settings
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records or uninstall the App to remove all data
  • Export: Currently, the App does not provide a data export feature

Because we do not store your data on our servers, we cannot access, retrieve, or delete your data on your behalf.

7. Data Retention

Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. When you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be deleted from your device (unless your device's operating system has created a backup).

8. International Data Transfers

Since your data never leaves your device through the App, there are no international data transfers to speak of. Your data stays on your device regardless of your location.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If we make material changes to how we handle user information (such as adding server-based features), we will notify users through the App or via the app store listing.

10. Permissions

Attendio may request the following device permissions:

  • Storage: To save your attendance data locally on your device
  • Notifications: To remind you to mark attendance (if you enable this feature)

These permissions are used solely for the App's core functionality and not for data collection or sharing.

11. Cookies and Tracking

Attendio does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any other tracking technologies. The App does not track your behavior or collect usage analytics.

13. Legal Compliance

While we do not collect or store your data, we are committed to complying with applicable privacy laws and regulations, including:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for users in California
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 for users in India
  • Other applicable local privacy laws

14. Data Breach Notification

Since your data is stored only on your device and we have no access to it, we cannot be responsible for data breaches on your device. If your device is lost, stolen, or compromised, please take appropriate security measures such as remotely wiping your device if possible.

15. Your Consent

By using Attendio, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Summary: Attendio respects your privacy completely. Your attendance data is yours alone, stored only on your device. We have no access to it, we don't collect it, and we don't share it. The App works entirely offline to keep your information private and secure.
